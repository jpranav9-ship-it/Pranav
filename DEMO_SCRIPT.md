# AEO Prospect Intelligence — 90-second demo

## 0–10s — The problem

"This is how I was doing prospecting for SemAI every day. I had a company list, then I would open LinkedIn, find the right marketing person, research their profile and figure out why I should reach out."

## 10–20s — Why it was painful

"The company wasn't the hard part. Finding the right person and doing the research before the outreach was taking hours."

## 20–35s — Start the product

Open the product and enter a real target company.

Say:

"So I built AEO Prospect Intelligence. I enter the company and let it do the first layer of prospect research for me."

## 35–55s — Show the results

Point to:

- Who to reach
- Why this person
- AEO context
- Outreach angle
- Source/evidence

Say:

"It gives me a few relevant marketing people, why they look relevant, how AEO could connect to their role, and an angle I can use to start the conversation."

## 55–70s — Take action

Click the source/profile link and save one prospect.

Say:

"If I want to come back to someone later, I can save the prospect to my workspace."

## 70–82s — Show workspace

Open the dashboard.

Show saved prospects and recent company research.

Say:

"Now the research doesn't disappear after one search. I can come back to my saved prospects and previous company research."

## 82–90s — Close

"This started as a problem I personally had while selling an AEO product. Now I'm testing whether other AEO/GEO teams have the same problem and would actually pay to solve it."

## Recording notes

- Use a real company.
- Keep the browser clean.
- Do not explain the tech stack unless asked.
- Do not over-edit the video.
- Keep the real product visible most of the time.
- One take is better than a polished fake demo.
